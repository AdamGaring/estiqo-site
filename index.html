Every property

Each property gets its own hub.

Open a property and it's already organised — the loan, the lease, the people, the paperwork, and what's coming up next.

  • Cash flow — rent and repayments feed in from the lease and loan you've already entered.
  • Documents & receipts — snap a photo or import a file, filed to the right property.
  • Key dates — lease renewals, fixed-rate expiry, insurance and rates, with reminders.
  • Contacts — property manager, tenants and tradies, one tap from a call.
  • Leases & loans — every tenancy and every loan, with the full history as things change.
  • Photos — up to 30 per property, swipeable like a real estate listing.

Getting started

Start with one property. Add the rest whenever.

You don't need last year's paperwork to begin. Enter a property, bring across the loan and lease, and Estiqo starts tracking from that day forward.

    Check in whenever

    Yield, equity and cash flow at a glance — and a full EOFY pack when tax time comes around.
